Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Puhu Studios ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use our mobile games, including Solimage — Image Puzzle (the "Games"), and our website games.puhustudios.com.

1. Information We Collect

Our Games do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or contact details to play. The following information may be collected automatically:

  • Device information — device model, operating system version, language, country/region, and app version.
  • Advertising identifiers — such as Apple's IDFA (only with your permission, see Section 4) or a resettable advertising ID, used by our advertising partners.
  • Ad interaction data — which ads were shown and whether they were viewed or tapped.
  • Purchase information — when you make an in-app purchase, the transaction is processed by Apple. We receive a confirmation of the purchase but never your payment card details.
  • Gameplay data — your progress, levels completed, and in-game currency, stored locally on your device.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate the Games and deliver purchased content;
  • To display advertising, which helps keep the Games free to play;
  • To fix bugs, prevent fraud, and improve stability and gameplay;
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Advertising Partners

The Games display ads served by third-party advertising networks. These partners may collect device identifiers and ad interaction data, acting as independent data controllers, to serve and measure ads. We encourage you to review their privacy policies. Where required, ads are served in a non-personalized (contextual) mode.

4. Your Choices & App Tracking Transparency

  • iOS: Under Apple's App Tracking Transparency, we will ask for your permission before tracking. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. You may also enable "Limit Ad Tracking" or reset your identifier.
  • Personalized ads: Declining tracking means you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized.
  • Local data: You can delete all locally stored game data by uninstalling the app.

5. Children's Privacy

Our Games are not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data Retention & Security

Gameplay data lives on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app. Data held by our partners is retained according to their published policies. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the limited data we handle.

7. International Transfers

Our partners may process data on servers located in other countries. Where required, appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) are used.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (e.g., the EU/EEA under GDPR, California under CCPA/CPRA, or Türkiye under KVKK), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal information, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Material changes will be highlighted in the app or on our website.
