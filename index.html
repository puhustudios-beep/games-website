Party Game

Guess Words: UpDown

🔄 Played in landscape — hold the phone up and go!

Split into teams, pick your categories, and race the clock. Teammates describe the big word on screen — guess it and tilt up, stuck and tilt down. Loudest team wins!

  • 🎉 The perfect party game for friends & family
  • 📱 Tilt up for correct, tilt down to pass — hands-free scoring
  • 🗂️ Tons of categories — movies, office, wedding, TV & more
  • ⚙️ Custom rounds, timers, difficulty and scoring
