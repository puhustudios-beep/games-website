Solimage

Flip, place, and complete gorgeous photo puzzles of the world's most iconic landmarks. Every solved picture unlocks the story behind it — from the Colosseum to the Sagrada Família.

  • 🌍 Travel the world country by country

02 — Word Puzzle

Word Association: Groups

Every board hides a category — Winter, School, Summer — and every word on it belongs to that category. Tap letters to fill the blanks, follow the clues, and complete the whole group before your lives run out.

  • 🔗 One category, every word connected
  • 🎨 Matching colors reveal repeated letters
  • ❤️ Three lives — think before you tap
  • 🛠️ Boosters for when a word just won't come

03 — Party Game

Guess Words: UpDown

🔄 Played in landscape — hold the phone up and go

Split into teams, pick your categories, and race the clock. Teammates describe the big word on screen — guess it and tilt up, stuck and tilt down. Loudest team wins.

  • 🎉 Built for friends & family nights
  • 📱 Tilt up or down — hands-free scoring
  • 🗂️ Movies, office, wedding, TV & more
  • ⚙️ Custom rounds, timers & difficulty

04 — Party Game

Forbidden Words Game: TabuTabu

The classic word-describing challenge, reimagined. Get your team to say the word on the card — but every card hides five forbidden words you're not allowed to use. Swipe right for a hit, left if you slip, up to pass.

  • 🚫 Five forbidden words on every card
  • 👆 Swipe to score — fast and intuitive
  • ⚡ Power-ups: extra time, double points, new card
  • 📊 Round summaries with every word you played
