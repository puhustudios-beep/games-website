Privacy Policy

Photo Finder: Reverse Image
Effective date: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Puhu Studios ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use the Photo Finder: Reverse Image mobile app (the "App").

1. What the App Does With Your Photo

When you choose a photo to search:

  • Location and device metadata (EXIF data) is removed from the photo on your device, before it is ever sent anywhere.
  • The photo is sent to our backend, which temporarily stores it only for as long as the search is running — typically a few seconds — and then automatically deletes it.
  • To perform the search, the photo (and, if you provided them, your search details) is sent to a third-party image and web search provider that returns matching pages back to us. That provider processes the image to perform the search but is not authorized by us to use it for any other purpose.
  • We do not keep a copy of your photo after the search completes, and we do not build a profile or history of the photos you have searched.

2. Information We Collect

CategoryWhat it isWhy
Your photo The image you choose to search To perform the reverse image search — deleted immediately after
Search details (optional) Name, city, employer/school, or usernames you choose to type in To search a small number of platforms by text — used only for that search, not stored as a profile

The App does not require you to create an account. You are never asked for your name or email address to use the App — the name field, if you fill it in, is used only as a search term.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

  • Image and web search provider — receives the photo (via a temporary, non-public link) and any search terms you provide, in order to return search results. It does not receive your identity, payment information, or App Store account.
  • Cloud infrastructure provider — briefly and temporarily hosts your photo while a search is in progress; the file is deleted automatically once the search finishes.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription status and entitlements on our behalf. It receives a device/app-specific identifier and purchase data, but never your payment card details.
  • Apple — processes all payments through your App Store account. We never see or receive your payment card details.

4. What We Don't Do

  • We do not show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not perform facial recognition or biometric identification.
  • We do not permanently store the photos you search.

5. Your Choices

  • Before every search, the App asks you to confirm the photo is your own or one you have permission to search.
  • You can delete all locally stored app data at any time by uninstalling the App.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID account settings at any time.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at, and is not intended for use by, anyone under 17 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the App or provided us information, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

7. Data Retention & Security

Photos are deleted automatically once a search completes, typically within seconds. We do not keep search history tied to your device. Subscription and device-identifier data is retained by our providers (Apple, RevenueCat) according to their own policies for as long as needed to provide the service. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the limited data we handle.

8. International Transfers

Our service providers may process data on servers located in other countries. Where required, appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) are used.
